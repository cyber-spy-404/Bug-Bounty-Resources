## 🛡️ Responsible Disclosure & Ethical Standards

When participating in bug bounty programs or conducting authorized security assessments, contributors and readers must adhere to the following principles:

1. **Strict Adherence to Scope:**
   - Always verify and re-verify the active program policy and scope boundaries before initiating any traffic.
   - Out-of-scope assets, third-party services, and unlisted subdomains are strictly off-limits.
   - If an asset is ambiguous, clarify with the program team before proceeding.

2. **Non-Destructive Testing:**
   - Do not perform actions that could impair the availability, stability, or integrity of services.
   - Avoid Denial-of-Service (DoS/DDoS) testing, resource exhaustion, or aggressive automated flooding unless explicitly requested in a private engagement.
   - Cease testing immediately upon verifying that a vulnerability exists (Proof-of-Concept), rather than exploring the maximum possible depth of compromise.

3. **Data Privacy and Protection:**
   - Never access, download, view, modify, or exfiltrate sensitive personal data (PII), payment card information, health records, or private company intellectual property.
   - Always conduct testing using your own test accounts created specifically for the assessment.
   - If user data is inadvertently accessed during verification, stop immediately, securely purge local temporary files, and notify the security team in your disclosure report.

4. **Coordinated Vulnerability Disclosure:**
   - Report findings directly to the affected organization via their official vulnerability disclosure program (VDP) or bug bounty platform (e.g., HackerOne, Bugcrowd, Intigriti).
   - Maintain confidentiality and do not publicly disclose details, proof-of-concepts, or technical writeups until the vulnerability has been resolved and the vendor has granted explicit permission for coordinated public disclosure.
